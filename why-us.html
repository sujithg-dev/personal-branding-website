27 Years of Practice

When it comes to labour laws, we are one of the most experienced. Labour laws statutes under the Central Government, under the various State Governments & all those under the Local Municipal Bodies have been given countless man-hours of study over the 27 years of our practice. Through all these years, we have also tracked each law through each update & review that it has been subject to by its respective authority. The countless disputes & challenges filed in the court of these laws have also been religiously scrutinised by us. This has equipped us with an immaculate understanding of each law and has helped us in delivering the correct advisory to all our clients.

Trained & Pro-Active Staff

Our intricate understanding of the multiple layers of labour law compliance has been rightly harnessed to develop effective training programs for all our employees. Aimed at entry level, middle & senior executive level, our training programs have been of immense value on a knowledge level. Our staff is one the most proactive & is the best in advising, documenting & maintaining records under all labour law enactments. Backed with our in-house proprietary software, our staff manages more than 5,000 provident fund accounts, more than 7,000 ESIC accounts and the payroll of more than 2000 employees across India.

Pan-India Presence

We understand the wide reach of today's businesses. That's why, we have an extensive network to cater to all your business's needs across India. We thus serve you as the single point for all your labour law & allied needs anywhere across the country.

Turnkey Solutions

In today's highly competitive environment, it is most desired that the company concentrates all its energy on its core strengths. From obtaining of all business & labour licences, their periodic Com Digitalpliance up-to any grievance redressed, we handle the full span and as your labour law & human capital partner, take ownership of labour law & other allied processes thus helping you to concentrate on your business.

120+ Clients

We serve some of the biggest Indian brands & MNCs that are spread across industries such as retail, Logistics, Constructions, Finance, Insurance, Hotel Industry, Institutions, Entertainment, Banking, Pharmaceuticals, Engineering, Information Technology, BPO's, etc.
