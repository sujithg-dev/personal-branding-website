About US

Sujith Groups, is a full spectrum labour law consulting & human resource advisory firm.


The company was started, as a proprietary firm, in 1999 by Sujith and The Company today employs more than 25 people and is the leading firm for Labour Law Consulting & Audit services. On the human resources side, the company's services include payroll management, contract staffing & recruitment consulting.


We believe in the essence of 'partnerships'. In our opinion, nothing is impossible if one has the right partner. Our relationship, with all of our clients, partners, business associates & vendors, averages more than 25 years and is the single most valuable asset that we enjoy. This, we believe, wouldn't have been possible without the support that we have received from all our clients & partners. In return, we have always laid high emphasis on maintaining an excellent standard of service.


As your labour law & human capital partner, we work with you and take ownership of labour law & other allied processes and thus help you to concentrate on your business. With over 27+ years of practice, we have gained immense knowledge & insights of different sectors & industries. Today, we serve 120+ clients across industries such as retail, Logistics, Constructions, Finance, Insurance, Hotel Industry, Institutions, Entertainment, Banking, Pharmaceuticals, Engineering, Information Technology, BPO's, etc. We are headquartered in Chennai and, through our network of associates, serve the entire geography of India.
