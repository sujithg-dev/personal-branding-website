About US


Sujith Groups, is a full spectrum labour law consulting & human resource advisory firm. The company was started, as a proprietary firm, in 1999 by Sujith and The Company today employs more than 25 people and is the leading firm for Labour Law Consulting & Audit services. On the human resources side, the company's services include payroll management, contract staffing & recruitment consulting.


We believe in the essence of 'partnerships'. In our opinion, nothing is impossible if one has the right partner. Our relationship, with all of our clients, partners, business associates & vendors, averages more than 25 years and is the single most valuable asset that we enjoy. This, we believe, wouldn't have been possible without the support that we have received from all our clients & partners. In return, we have always laid high emphasis on maintaining an excellent standard of service.
